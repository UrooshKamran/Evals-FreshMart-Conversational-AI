FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 1

Q: What are FreshMart delivery hours?
A: FreshMart offers three delivery slots: Morning (9am-12pm), Afternoon (1pm-5pm), and Evening (6pm-9pm). You can select your preferred slot at checkout.

Q: Is same-day delivery available?
A: Yes! Orders placed before 2pm qualify for same-day delivery. Orders placed after 2pm will be delivered the next day.
