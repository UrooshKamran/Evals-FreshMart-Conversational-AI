FreshMart Returns and Refunds Policy

Fresh Produce Returns:
Fresh fruits and vegetables can be returned within 24 hours of delivery if they are damaged, spoiled, or not as described. Simply contact our support team with a photo and we will arrange a replacement or full refund.

Packaged Items Returns:
Packaged, non-perishable items can be returned within 7 days of delivery provided they are unopened and in original condition. Items must be returned in their original packaging.

Dairy and Frozen Items:
Dairy products and frozen items follow the same 24-hour policy as fresh produce due to their perishable nature.

How to Initiate a Return:
1. Contact FreshMart support via chat, phone, or email
2. Provide your order number and reason for return
3. Our team will arrange a pickup or ask you to dispose of the item
4. Refund is processed within 3-5 business days to your original payment method

Non-Returnable Items:
Items that have been opened, partially consumed, or damaged by the customer are not eligible for return.

Refund Timeline:
Credit/Debit card refunds: 3-5 business days
PayPal refunds: 1-3 business days
Cash on delivery orders: Store credit issued immediately
