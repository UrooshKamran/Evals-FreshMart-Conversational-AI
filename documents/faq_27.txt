FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 27

Q: What coffee brewing methods is FreshMart ground coffee suitable for?
A: FreshMart ground coffee is a medium grind suitable for drip coffee makers, French press, pour-over, and espresso machines with some adjustment. It is not suitable for instant coffee preparation.

Q: Does FreshMart sell instant coffee?
A: Currently FreshMart stocks ground coffee only. Instant coffee options are being evaluated for future addition.
