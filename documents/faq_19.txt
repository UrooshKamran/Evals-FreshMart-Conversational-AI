FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 19

Q: Is dark chocolate healthy?
A: Dark chocolate with 70% or more cocoa content is rich in antioxidants, magnesium, and iron. It may support heart health and mood. However, it should be consumed in moderation due to its calorie content.

Q: What percentage of cocoa is in FreshMart dark chocolate?
A: FreshMart dark chocolate bars contain 70% cocoa content. They are made with natural cocoa and minimal additives.
