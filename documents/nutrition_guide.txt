FreshMart Nutrition Guide

Healthy Eating Tips from FreshMart

Fruits and Vegetables:
Aim to eat at least 5 portions of fruits and vegetables per day. A portion is approximately 80g of fresh produce. FreshMart makes it easy to hit your daily target with our fresh and affordable produce selection.

High-Fiber Foods:
Whole wheat bread, broccoli, carrots, and mixed nuts are all excellent sources of dietary fiber. Fiber helps maintain a healthy digestive system and keeps you feeling full longer.

Protein Sources:
Greek yogurt, mixed nuts, and cheddar cheese are good protein sources available at FreshMart. Protein is essential for muscle repair and immune function.

Healthy Fats:
Almond milk, mixed nuts, and dark chocolate all contain healthy unsaturated fats. These support heart health and brain function.

Hydration:
Adults should drink at least 8 glasses of water per day. FreshMart stocks mineral water, sparkling water, and herbal teas to help you stay hydrated.

Low-Calorie Options:
Cucumber, spinach, sparkling water, and rice crackers are all low-calorie options available at FreshMart. Great for weight management.

Vitamins and Minerals:
- Vitamin C: Oranges, bell peppers, strawberries, broccoli
- Vitamin A: Carrots, spinach, mango
- Calcium: Full cream milk, cheddar cheese, Greek yogurt
- Iron: Spinach, dark chocolate
- Potassium: Banana, potato
