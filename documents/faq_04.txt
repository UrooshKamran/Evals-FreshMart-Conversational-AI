FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 4

Q: Are FreshMart products fresh?
A: All FreshMart products are sourced fresh daily. Fruits and vegetables are inspected before dispatch. Dairy and bakery items have clear expiry dates on the packaging.

Q: Where does FreshMart source its produce?
A: We source from a combination of local farms within 50km of the city and trusted international suppliers for products not grown locally.
