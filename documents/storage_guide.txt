FreshMart Product Storage Guide

Proper storage ensures your groceries stay fresh for longer.

Fruits:
- Apples: Refrigerate for up to 3 weeks or store at room temperature for 1 week
- Bananas: Store at room temperature. Do not refrigerate (skin will blacken)
- Mangoes: Ripen at room temperature, then refrigerate for up to 5 days
- Strawberries: Refrigerate immediately. Consume within 2-3 days
- Watermelon: Whole at room temperature. Cut pieces refrigerated for 3 days
- Grapes: Refrigerate unwashed. Consume within 1 week
- Oranges: Room temperature for 1 week or refrigerate for 3 weeks
- Pineapple: Room temperature until ripe, then refrigerate

Vegetables:
- Tomatoes: Room temperature. Do not refrigerate (loses flavor)
- Potatoes: Dark, cool, dry place. Not in refrigerator
- Onions: Cool, dry, ventilated area. Away from potatoes
- Spinach: Refrigerate immediately. Use within 3 days
- Carrots: Refrigerate in a sealed bag for up to 2 weeks
- Broccoli: Refrigerate. Use within 3-5 days
- Cucumber: Refrigerate. Use within 1 week
- Bell Peppers: Refrigerate. Use within 1 week

Dairy:
- Milk: Refrigerate immediately. Use before expiry date
- Cheese: Refrigerate wrapped. Use within 7 days of opening
- Yogurt: Refrigerate. Use before expiry date
- Butter: Refrigerate or freeze. Use within 1 month

Bakery:
- Bread: Room temperature for 3 days or freeze for up to 3 months
- Croissants and rolls: Best consumed on day of purchase
- Muffins and cinnamon rolls: Room temperature for 2 days
