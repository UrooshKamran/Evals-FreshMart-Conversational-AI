FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 29

Q: How spicy are the bell peppers?
A: FreshMart bell peppers are sweet peppers with zero heat. They come in red, yellow, and green varieties. Red peppers are the sweetest, green are slightly bitter. None of them are hot or spicy.

Q: Does FreshMart sell chili peppers?
A: Currently FreshMart stocks sweet bell peppers only. Chili peppers and hot peppers are being considered for future catalog additions.
