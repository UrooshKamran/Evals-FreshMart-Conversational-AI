FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 14

Q: What is the difference between full cream and skimmed milk?
A: Full cream milk contains 3.5% fat and has a richer, creamier taste. Skimmed milk has less than 0.5% fat and fewer calories but retains the same vitamins and minerals. Choose based on your dietary needs.

Q: How long does milk last after opening?
A: Opened milk should be consumed within 3-5 days when refrigerated. Always check the expiry date on the packaging.
