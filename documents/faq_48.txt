FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 48

Q: Does FreshMart price match?
A: FreshMart does not offer formal price matching. However, our prices are reviewed regularly to ensure competitiveness. If you find a significantly lower price elsewhere for the same product, contact support and we will do our best to help.

Q: Why are some FreshMart products more expensive than supermarkets?
A: FreshMart prioritizes freshness, quality, and convenience. Our products are sourced from premium suppliers and delivered to your door. The small premium reflects the quality and service.
