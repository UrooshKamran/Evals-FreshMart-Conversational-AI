FreshMart Payment Methods

Accepted Payment Methods:
- Visa and Mastercard credit cards
- Visa and Mastercard debit cards
- PayPal
- Cash on Delivery (COD)

Not Accepted:
- Personal or business cheques
- Cryptocurrency
- Wire transfers

Cash on Delivery:
COD is available for orders up to $100. The delivery driver carries change for amounts up to $150. For larger orders, please use an electronic payment method.

Payment Security:
All online payments are processed through our secure payment gateway with 256-bit SSL encryption. FreshMart does not store your full card details on our servers.

Saving Payment Methods:
You can save up to 3 payment methods in your FreshMart account for faster checkout. Saved cards are tokenized for security.

Invoice and Receipts:
A digital receipt is emailed to you after every order. You can also download invoices from your account order history.
