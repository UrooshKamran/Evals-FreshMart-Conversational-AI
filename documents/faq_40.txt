FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 40

Q: What is the delivery area for FreshMart?
A: FreshMart delivers to all residential zones within the city limits. We do not currently deliver to areas outside the city or to PO boxes. Enter your address at checkout to confirm delivery availability.

Q: Will FreshMart expand its delivery area?
A: FreshMart is actively working on expanding to suburban areas. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when delivery reaches your area.
