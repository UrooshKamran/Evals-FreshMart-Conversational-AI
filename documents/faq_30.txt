FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 30

Q: What is sourdough bread and how is it different from regular bread?
A: Sourdough bread is made using a natural fermentation process with wild yeast and bacteria instead of commercial yeast. This gives it a distinctive tangy flavor, chewy texture, and longer shelf life. It is also easier to digest than regular bread.

Q: Is sourdough bread healthier than white bread?
A: Sourdough has a lower glycemic index than white bread and contains beneficial bacteria from fermentation. It is a healthier choice for most people.
