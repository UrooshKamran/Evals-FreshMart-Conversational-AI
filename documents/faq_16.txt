FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 16

Q: What is the best way to store cheese?
A: Cheese should be refrigerated and wrapped in wax paper or plastic wrap. Once opened, cheddar lasts up to 7 days and mozzarella should be consumed within 3 days. Keep away from strongly scented foods.

Q: Can I freeze cheese?
A: Hard cheeses like cheddar can be frozen for up to 3 months. Soft cheeses like mozzarella and cream cheese do not freeze well and may become crumbly.
