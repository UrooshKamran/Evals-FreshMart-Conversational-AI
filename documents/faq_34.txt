FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 34

Q: What is the FreshMart customer support phone number?
A: FreshMart customer support is available at 1-800-FRESHMART (1-800-373-7462). Support hours are 8am to 10pm daily. You can also reach us via live chat on the website or email at support@freshmart.com.

Q: How quickly does FreshMart respond to emails?
A: FreshMart aims to respond to all emails within 4 hours during business hours. For urgent issues, we recommend using phone or live chat.
