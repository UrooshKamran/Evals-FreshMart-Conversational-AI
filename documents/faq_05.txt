FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 5

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Yes. Fresh produce can be returned within 24 hours if damaged or spoiled. Packaged items can be returned within 7 days if unopened. Contact support with your order number and a photo.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Card refunds take 3-5 business days. PayPal refunds take 1-3 business days. Cash on delivery orders receive immediate store credit.
