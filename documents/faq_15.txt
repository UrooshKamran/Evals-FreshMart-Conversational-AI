FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 15

Q: What makes Greek yogurt different from regular yogurt?
A: Greek yogurt is strained to remove liquid whey, resulting in a thicker consistency and higher protein content. It has a tangy flavor and is great for breakfast, dips, and cooking.

Q: Can I use Greek yogurt as a substitute for sour cream?
A: Yes! Greek yogurt is an excellent, healthier substitute for sour cream in most recipes. It has a similar tangy flavor and creamy texture.
