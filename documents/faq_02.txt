FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 2

Q: What is the minimum order value?
A: There is no minimum order value at FreshMart. However, orders below 5 will incur a .00 delivery fee. Orders above 5 receive free delivery.

Q: Can I schedule a delivery in advance?
A: Yes, you can schedule deliveries up to 7 days in advance by selecting your preferred date and time slot at checkout.
