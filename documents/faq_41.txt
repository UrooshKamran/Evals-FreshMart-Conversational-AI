FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 41

Q: How is FreshMart coffee packaged?
A: FreshMart ground coffee comes in a resealable 200g bag to preserve freshness. Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Once opened, consume within 2 weeks for best flavor.

Q: Does FreshMart sell coffee beans?
A: Currently FreshMart stocks pre-ground coffee only. Whole bean coffee options are being considered for future addition.
