FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 3

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a real-time tracking link. You can also check your order status in your FreshMart account under Order History.

Q: What happens if I miss my delivery?
A: If you are not home, the driver will attempt to leave the order in a safe place if you have pre-authorized this. Otherwise, a redelivery will be attempted the next business day free of charge.
