FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 45

Q: How does FreshMart ensure food safety?
A: FreshMart follows strict food safety standards. All products are stored at correct temperatures throughout the supply chain. Delivery vehicles are temperature-controlled. Staff undergo regular food safety training.

Q: What temperature are chilled products kept at?
A: Dairy and other chilled products are kept at 4 degrees Celsius or below throughout storage and delivery. Ice packs are included for deliveries during hot weather.
