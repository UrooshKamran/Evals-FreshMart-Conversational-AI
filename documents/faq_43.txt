FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 43

Q: Can I get FreshMart products delivered to my workplace?
A: Yes! FreshMart delivers to workplaces within the city. Enter your office address at checkout. Please ensure someone is available to receive the delivery during business hours.

Q: Does FreshMart do corporate accounts?
A: Yes! FreshMart offers corporate accounts for businesses. Corporate accounts receive dedicated account management, invoicing, and volume discounts. Contact corporate@freshmart.com for details.
