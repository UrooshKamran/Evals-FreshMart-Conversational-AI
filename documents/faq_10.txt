FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 10

Q: Can I order for someone else?
A: Yes. You can enter a different delivery address at checkout. The recipient does not need a FreshMart account to receive the order.

Q: Can I send a FreshMart order as a gift?
A: Yes! Select the gift option at checkout to include a personalized message with the delivery. The price receipt will not be included with gift orders.
