FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 22

Q: Does FreshMart deliver on weekends?
A: Yes! FreshMart delivers 7 days a week including weekends and most public holidays. Delivery slots and availability may vary on major holidays. Check the app for holiday schedules.

Q: Are there extra charges for weekend delivery?
A: No. Delivery charges are the same on weekdays and weekends. Free delivery applies to all orders above 5 regardless of the day.
