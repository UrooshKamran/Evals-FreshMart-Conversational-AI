FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 31

Q: What vegetables go well together in a stir-fry?
A: For a great stir-fry, try combining bell peppers, broccoli, carrots, onions, and spinach from FreshMart. Add garlic and a sauce of your choice. These vegetables cook quickly and retain their color and nutrients.

Q: What vegetables are good for soup?
A: Potatoes, carrots, onions, tomatoes, and spinach from FreshMart make excellent soup bases. They provide depth of flavor and nutrition.
