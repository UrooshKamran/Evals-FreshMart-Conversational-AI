FreshMart How to Order Guide

Placing an Order with Shabo (FreshMart Virtual Assistant):

Step 1 - Browse Products:
Ask Shabo what products are available. You can ask by category (e.g. "What fruits do you have?") or by product name (e.g. "Do you have mozzarella?").

Step 2 - Add to Cart:
Tell Shabo what you want to add. Examples:
- "Add 2 apples to my cart"
- "I want a loaf of sourdough bread"
- "Put 3 bottles of mineral water in my cart"

Step 3 - Review Cart:
Ask Shabo to show your cart at any time. Example: "What's in my cart?" or "Show me my order."

Step 4 - Apply Promotions:
Shabo automatically applies available promotions. You can ask "What deals are available?" to see current offers.

Step 5 - Checkout:
When ready, tell Shabo you want to checkout. Shabo will show you a full order summary including items, quantities, prices, promotions applied, delivery fee, and total.

Step 6 - Confirm:
Confirm your order and provide delivery slot preference (morning, afternoon, or evening).

Modifying Your Order:
You can add or remove items at any time before confirming. Example: "Remove the bananas" or "Add one more coffee."

Cancelling an Order:
Orders can be cancelled within 30 minutes of placement for a full refund.
