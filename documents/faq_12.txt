FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 12

Q: Is almond milk a good dairy substitute?
A: Almond milk is an excellent dairy-free alternative. It is lower in calories than cow's milk and suitable for vegans and those with lactose intolerance. It does not contain as much protein as cow's milk.

Q: What dairy-free options does FreshMart offer?
A: FreshMart offers almond milk as a dairy-free alternative. All fruits, vegetables, and most snacks are also dairy-free. Check allergen information for details.
