FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 20

Q: Can I order a specific quantity of vegetables?
A: Yes. Most vegetables are sold by the kilogram or by piece. You can specify the quantity when ordering. For example, ask for 2 kg of tomatoes or 3 cucumbers.

Q: Are vegetables pre-cut or whole?
A: FreshMart delivers vegetables whole and uncut to maintain freshness. Preparation is left to the customer.
