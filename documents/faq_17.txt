FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 17

Q: Are FreshMart nuts roasted or raw?
A: The mixed nuts from FreshMart are available in both raw and dry-roasted varieties. The standard pack is dry-roasted and unsalted. Check the product label for details.

Q: How should I store nuts?
A: Store nuts in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks, or refrigerate for up to 3 months. Nuts can also be frozen for up to a year.
