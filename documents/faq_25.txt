FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 25

Q: What is the weight of a FreshMart watermelon?
A: FreshMart watermelons typically weigh between 5 and 7 kilograms. They are whole, seedless watermelons. The exact weight may vary slightly.

Q: How do I know if a watermelon is ripe?
A: A ripe watermelon has a yellow spot on one side (where it rested on the ground), sounds hollow when tapped, and feels heavy for its size. FreshMart selects ripe watermelons before dispatch.
