FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 35

Q: Is FreshMart environmentally friendly?
A: FreshMart is committed to sustainability. We use biodegradable packaging for most products, work with local farms to reduce transportation emissions, and have a food waste reduction program where unsold produce is donated to local food banks.

Q: Does FreshMart use plastic bags?
A: FreshMart uses minimal plastic. Fresh produce is packed in biodegradable bags and paper. We encourage customers to reuse packaging where possible.
