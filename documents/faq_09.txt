FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 9

Q: What is the FreshMart loyalty program?
A: FreshMart Rewards members earn 1 point for every  spent. 100 points =  discount on future orders. Sign up for free on the website or app.

Q: How do I redeem loyalty points?
A: At checkout, you will see your available points balance. You can choose to redeem some or all points towards your order total.
