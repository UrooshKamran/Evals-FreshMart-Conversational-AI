FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 28

Q: Are FreshMart granola bars gluten-free?
A: FreshMart granola bars contain oats which may contain gluten. They are not certified gluten-free. Customers with celiac disease should consult the product label or contact support.

Q: Are granola bars suitable for children?
A: Yes, granola bars are a great snack for children. They provide energy from oats and honey. Check for nut content if your child has a nut allergy.
