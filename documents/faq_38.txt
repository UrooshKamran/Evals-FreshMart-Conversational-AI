FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 38

Q: Does FreshMart sell eggs?
A: Yes, eggs are available at FreshMart as part of bundle deals and as a standalone product. Free-range eggs are available. Check the app or website for current availability and pricing.

Q: Are the eggs free-range?
A: FreshMart offers both standard and free-range egg options. Free-range eggs are slightly more expensive but support better animal welfare standards.
