FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 13

Q: How do I store bananas?
A: Bananas should be stored at room temperature. Do not refrigerate bananas as the cold causes the skin to blacken. Once ripe, you can refrigerate them for 1-2 extra days.

Q: Why do my bananas ripen so fast?
A: Bananas emit ethylene gas which speeds up ripening. Store them away from other fruits to slow down ripening of nearby produce.
