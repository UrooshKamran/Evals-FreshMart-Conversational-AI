FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 24

Q: Does FreshMart have a mobile app?
A: Yes! The FreshMart app is available on iOS and Android. It offers the same features as the website plus push notifications for delivery updates, exclusive app-only deals, and faster checkout.

Q: Is ordering through the app the same price as the website?
A: Yes, prices are identical on the app and website. Occasionally, the app offers exclusive discounts not available on the website.
