FreshMart Delivery Policy

Delivery Slots:
- Morning slot: 9am to 12pm
- Afternoon slot: 1pm to 5pm
- Evening slot: 6pm to 9pm

Delivery Fees:
- Standard delivery fee: $3.00
- Free delivery on all orders above $25.00
- Express delivery (within 2 hours): $7.00 additional charge

Same-Day Delivery:
Orders placed before 2:00pm are eligible for same-day delivery. Orders placed after 2:00pm will be delivered the next day unless express delivery is selected.

Delivery Coverage:
FreshMart delivers to all residential zones within city limits. We do not currently deliver to PO boxes or industrial areas.

Delivery Tracking:
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also track your order through the FreshMart app or website.

Failed Deliveries:
If no one is available to receive the order, the delivery driver will leave it in a safe place if instructed. Otherwise, a redelivery will be attempted the next day at no extra charge.
