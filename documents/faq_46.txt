FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 46

Q: Can I set up a recurring order?
A: Yes! FreshMart offers a subscription feature where you can set up weekly or bi-weekly recurring orders. Subscribers receive 5% off every order. Set up your subscription in your account settings.

Q: Can I pause or cancel my subscription?
A: Yes, you can pause or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings with no penalty. Changes must be made at least 24 hours before the next scheduled delivery.
