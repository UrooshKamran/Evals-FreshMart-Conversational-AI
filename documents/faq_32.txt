FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 32

Q: Can I order FreshMart products for a party or event?
A: Yes! FreshMart accepts bulk orders for events. For orders above 50, contact our corporate sales team for special pricing and dedicated delivery arrangements.

Q: How far in advance should I order for a party?
A: We recommend ordering at least 48 hours in advance for large party orders to ensure availability and timely delivery.
