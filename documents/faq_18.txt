FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 18

Q: What is the difference between sparkling water and mineral water?
A: Mineral water is still (non-carbonated) and contains natural minerals from the source. Sparkling water is carbonated (contains CO2 bubbles). Both are calorie-free and hydrating.

Q: Does FreshMart sell flavored water?
A: Currently FreshMart stocks plain mineral water and sparkling water. Flavored water options are being considered for future addition.
