FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 33

Q: Does FreshMart offer gift cards?
A: Yes! FreshMart gift cards are available in denominations of 0, 5, 0, and 00. They can be purchased online and redeemed at checkout. Gift cards are valid for 12 months.

Q: Can I use a gift card and a promo code together?
A: Yes. Gift cards and promo codes can both be applied to the same order. The gift card balance is deducted first, then the promo code discount.
