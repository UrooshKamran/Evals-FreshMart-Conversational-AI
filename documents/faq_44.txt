FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 44

Q: What are the ingredients in FreshMart salted chips?
A: FreshMart salted chips contain potatoes, sunflower oil, and sea salt. No artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives. Suitable for vegans.

Q: Are FreshMart chips gluten-free?
A: Yes, FreshMart salted chips are gluten-free. They are produced in a facility that also handles wheat products, so there may be traces of gluten. Check the label if you have severe celiac disease.
