FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 6

Q: Does FreshMart offer organic products?
A: FreshMart is working on expanding our organic range. Currently, our spinach, carrots, and apples are available in organic varieties on request. Contact support for availability.

Q: Are the fruits and vegetables washed before delivery?
A: Vegetables are inspected but not washed before delivery to preserve freshness. We recommend washing all produce before consumption.
