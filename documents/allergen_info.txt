FreshMart Allergen Information

FreshMart takes allergen safety very seriously. Below is allergen information for our product categories.

Dairy Allergens:
All dairy products (milk, cheese, yogurt, butter, cream cheese, sour cream) contain cow's milk. Customers with lactose intolerance or dairy allergies should avoid these products. Almond milk is a dairy-free alternative.

Gluten:
Bakery products including bread, croissants, bagels, dinner rolls, muffins, and cinnamon rolls contain gluten (wheat). Pretzels also contain gluten. Customers with celiac disease or gluten sensitivity should avoid these items.

Nuts:
Mixed nuts contain almonds, cashews, walnuts, and pistachios. Almond milk contains almonds. Dark chocolate may be processed in a facility that handles nuts. Granola bars may contain traces of nuts.

Eggs:
Some bakery items may contain eggs. Please check individual product labels.

Soy:
Some products may contain soy derivatives. Check individual labels.

Vegan Products:
The following products are suitable for vegans: all fruits, all vegetables, mineral water, sparkling water, orange juice, apple juice, green tea, almond milk, salted chips, rice crackers, gummy bears, pretzels.

If you have a severe allergy, please contact FreshMart support before placing your order so we can advise on safe products.
