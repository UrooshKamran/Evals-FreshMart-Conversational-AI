FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 23

Q: How do I cancel an order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 30 minutes of placement for a full refund. After 30 minutes, cancellation may not be possible if the order has been dispatched. Contact support immediately if you need to cancel.

Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A: Minor modifications can be made within 15 minutes of placing the order. Contact support as soon as possible. Once the order is being prepared, modifications may not be possible.
