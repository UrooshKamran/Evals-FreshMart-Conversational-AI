FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 47

Q: What are the benefits of green tea?
A: Green tea from FreshMart is rich in antioxidants called catechins, which support heart health and may reduce the risk of certain diseases. It contains caffeine for a gentle energy boost and L-theanine for calm focus. It supports metabolism and hydration.

Q: How should I prepare green tea?
A: Brew green tea in water heated to 80 degrees Celsius (not boiling). Steep for 2-3 minutes. Boiling water can make green tea bitter.
