FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 11

Q: What is the shelf life of bread from FreshMart?
A: White and whole wheat bread lasts 3 days at room temperature or up to 3 months if frozen. Sourdough lasts 4-5 days. Croissants and rolls are best on the day of purchase.

Q: Is the bread baked fresh?
A: Yes. FreshMart partners with local artisan bakeries. All bakery items are baked fresh daily and delivered the same day.
