FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 26

Q: Can I buy half a watermelon?
A: Currently FreshMart sells whole watermelons only. If a whole watermelon is too large, consider sharing with a neighbor or family. Cut watermelon should be refrigerated and consumed within 3 days.

Q: Does FreshMart sell pre-cut fruit?
A: Currently FreshMart sells whole fruits. Pre-cut fruit platters are being explored for future addition to the catalog.
