FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 42

Q: What makes FreshMart mozzarella good for pizza?
A: FreshMart mozzarella is fresh and high in moisture, which gives it that perfect melt and stretch when baked. It has a mild, milky flavor that complements pizza toppings without overpowering them.

Q: What is the difference between cheddar and mozzarella?
A: Cheddar is a harder, aged cheese with a sharp and tangy flavor. Mozzarella is a soft, fresh cheese with a mild and milky flavor. Cheddar is great for sandwiches and snacking while mozzarella is perfect for pizzas and salads.
