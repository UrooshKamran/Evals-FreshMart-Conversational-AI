FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 21

Q: What is the FreshMart referral program?
A: Refer a friend using your unique referral code and both you and your friend receive  credit when they place their first order. There is no limit to how many friends you can refer.

Q: Where do I find my referral code?
A: Your referral code is in your FreshMart account under the Referrals section. You can share it via link, SMS, or email.
