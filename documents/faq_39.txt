FreshMart Frequently Asked Questions - Part 39

Q: Can I speak to a human customer service agent?
A: Yes! While Shabo can handle most requests, you can always ask to speak with a human agent. Type 'connect me to an agent' and Shabo will transfer your chat or provide the phone number.

Q: What can Shabo help me with?
A: Shabo can help you browse products, add or remove items from your cart, check promotions, answer questions about delivery and returns, and complete your checkout.
